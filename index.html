Automatic sales & stock

Sales are recorded automatically when both buyer and seller confirm an accepted trade as completed. TEMO calculates cash and estimated barter value from the offer and listing price, records cost of goods from your listing cost per unit, and updates stock where stock tracking is enabled.

You do not need to enter the same sale here. Use the Trade Centre to confirm a real completed exchange. Add expenses separately because transport, labour and other costs may not be known by TEMO.
